Zalo Listing Bot — Portable (Windows)
=====================================

1. Giai nen folder nay vao vi tri bat ky (vi du D:\Apps\ZaloListingBot)

2. Chay Install.cmd
   - Tao config.ini / blocklist.csv neu chua co
   - Huong dan mo bot

   NEU BOT CRASH NGAY:
   - Chay Run-Bot-Debug.cmd (hien MsgBox + data\startup-error.log)
   - Hoac: AutoHotkey64.exe src\diag-startup.ahk

3. Danh sach nhom
   - Bot tu mo Alt+3 va doc toan bo nhom tren Zalo PC
   - 5 nhom output duoc khai bao tai [Groups] OutputGroups trong config.ini
   - Tat ca nhom con lai tu dong la input; khong dung groups.csv

4. Chay ZaloListingBot.exe
   - Bot tu cho Zalo, tu harvest + publish (AutoRunWatch=1)
   - Dung khan cap: Ctrl+Shift+K

5. (Tuy chon) Tu mo khi dang nhap Windows:
   setup\install-startup.cmd

Yeu cau:
- Windows 10/11
- Zalo PC da cai, account bot trong du nhom
- Khong can cai AutoHotkey neu da co ZaloListingBot.exe

Log / du lieu:
- data\listings\   — tin da harvest
- data\queue\      — hang doi publish
- data\media\      — archive anh

Sua cau hinh: config\config.ini
